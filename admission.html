INTRODUCTION

Punjab State Board of Technical Education and Industrial Training (PSBTE & IT) is an autonomous statutory authority created under The Punjab State Board of Technical Education & Industrial Training 1992 Act for regulating and controlling academic standards in Institutes of Technical Education and for making admissions & conducting examinations in Polytechnics and Industrial Training Institutes located in the state of Punjab.
